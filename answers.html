Common questions

Is an annual tag the same as six-year maintenance?

No. The annual visit inspects and tags. Six-year maintenance on many stored-pressure dry-chemical units is internal service. Hydrostatic testing is a pressure test on another interval. Ask which of those is due this visit.

Who licenses portable extinguisher companies in Texas?

The Texas State Fire Marshal registers companies with an ECR number and licenses technicians. Ask for both numbers. This site is not the fire marshal.

Does Dallas require annual service?

NFPA 10 and common occupancy rules expect a monthly visual check by the owner and annual maintenance. Inspectors can ask to see current tags. Confirm with your occupancy and Dallas Fire-Rescue fire prevention. This site does not interpret the code for your building.

Why is a recharge quote higher than the annual tag?

Recharge after use or low pressure is a different service. Ask which units need recharge versus tag-only. This site does not set prices.

Do I have to replace a unit that still shows pressure?

Ask whether it failed a required check, is past a manufacturer or hydro date, or is only recommended. Get that written on the ticket.
